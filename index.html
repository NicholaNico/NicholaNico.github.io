Research

Spectral and Additive Combinatorial Methods for Cycles and Absorbing Sets in Lifted-Product Quantum LDPC Codes

Aida Abiad, Nichola Castriota

How well a quantum LDPC code decodes at practical lengths depends on small structures in its Tanner graph: short cycles and absorbing sets. We build a spectral framework for lifted-product codes, the family behind most current quantum LDPC constructions. A Fourier block-diagonalisation reduces cycle counting to a sum of small Hermitian matrices. From it we get closed-form 4-cycle counts for generalised bicycle codes via additive energy, a Sidon-set criterion for girth 6, a Fourier count of (3,3) absorbing sets, and a lower bound on stopping-set size.
